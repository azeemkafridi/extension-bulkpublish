BulkPublish for Safari

Turn on the BulkPublish extension in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

The BulkPublish extension is on. Click the BulkPublish button in the Safari toolbar on any page to share it.

The BulkPublish extension is off. Turn it on in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Safari is open. To finish turning on the extension:

  1. In the menu bar, choose Safari › Settings…
  2. Click Extensions.
  3. Tick BulkPublish for Safari.

Not in the list? Quit Safari, open it again, and check once more.

After it is on, click the BulkPublish button in the Safari toolbar and sign in with your BulkPublish API key.
